<b>Glassware required :</b><br>
1)	Weighing balance<br>
2)	Conical flask (150 ml)<br>
3)	Measuring cylinder (10 ml, 100 ml)<br>
4)	Glass dropper<br>   
5)	Filtering flask<br>
6)	Water bath<br>
7)	Ice bath<br><br>

<b>Chemicals required :</b><br>
1)	Salicylic acid<br>
2)	Acetic Anhydride<br>
3)	Concentrated Sulfuric Acid<br>
4)	Ethanol<br>
5)	Distilled water.<br><br>

<b>Laboratory procedure :</b><br>
1)	Weigh 2.0 g of salicylic acid (Mol. Wt. 138.12 g/mol) in a watch glass and transfer it to a dry 150 ml conical flask.<br>
2)	Add 2.7 equivalents of acetic anhydride (Mol. Wt. 102.08 g/mol) using a measuring cylinder to the salicylic acid. Add 7-8 drops of concentrated sulfuric acid and stir until all salicylic acid is dissolved.<br>
3)	Leave the reaction mixture undisturbed for 20 minutes. A white solid should appear. <br>
4)	Add 50 ml water to the flask and swirl for two minutes and filter.<br>
5)	Collect the solid from the filter paper.<br><br>

<b>Recrystallization :</b><br>
1)	Dissolve the crude product in 7 ml ethanol in a beaker and add 15 ml of distilled water. Heat on water bath till you get a clear solution.<br>
2)	Allow the solution to cool in an ice bath without disturbing. Pure acetylsalicylic acid crystalizes.<br>
3)	Filter the pure product and allow it to dry. <br><br>

<b>Calculation for the amount of acetic anhydride needed for a given amount of salicylic acid :</b><br>

We have been asked to add 2.7 equivalents of acetic anhydride with respect to salicylic acid.<br>
Mol. Wt. of salicylic acid = 138.12 g/mol<br>
Mol. Wt. of acetic anhydride = 102.08 g/mol<br>
Density of acetic anhydride = 1.08 g/ml<br>

<b>Calculation of percentage yield of the prepared Aspirin :</b><br>
Mol. Wt. of Aspirin = 180.158 g/mol<br>
From the chemical equation we know that 1 mole of salicylic acid will give 1 mol of acetyl salicylic acid / Aspirin.<br>

<b>Calculation of theoretical yield of acetyl salicylic acid: </b><br>

Moles of salicylic acid taken = 0.0144 moles<br>
Hence, we should get 0.0144 moles of acetyl salicylic acid<br>
Theoretical yield = 0.0144 moles = 2.594 grams of acetyl salicylic acid.<br><br>
